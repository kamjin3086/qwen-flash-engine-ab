A few things worth knowing:

- **The scene is procedurally generated** on load — the terrain, tree placement, flowers, and petal seeds are randomized, so you'll get a slightly different garden each refresh.
- **Controls:** drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. The panel lets you switch **time of day** (with a smooth interpolated transition), toggle an **auto day cycle**, drifting **petals**, **auto-orbit**, and **bloom**.
- **Living details:** the pond water shimmers, koi swim in circles, petals fall and spin, fireflies fade in at dusk/night, and the sun/moon/stars move with the sky.
- **Performance:** all voxels are merged into per-color `InstancedMesh` batches, so it stays smooth even with tens of thousands of cubes. If your machine is modest, drop the petal count (`PC`) or shadow map size.
